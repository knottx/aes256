AES-256 Playground

Encrypt and decrypt text online with AES-256-GCM and a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 key derived from your passphrase. Everything runs locally in your browser and stays in memory, so nothing leaves this page.

How it works

  • Encrypt: enter plaintext and a passphrase to get Base64 ciphertext.
  • Decrypt: paste the Base64 ciphertext and the same passphrase to recover the text.
  • Authenticated encryption: a wrong passphrase or modified ciphertext is detected.

This is the live demo for package:aes256 on pub.dev (source on GitHub), a Dart and Flutter library for AES-256 encryption.
